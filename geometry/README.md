The original part files are preserved without geometry edits. Both models should be opened in SolidWorks, and the exported geometry can then be imported into ANSYS Mechanical. Refer to the normal- and shear-loading guides in [`docs/`](../docs/) for geometry, material regions, meshing and boundary conditions.
